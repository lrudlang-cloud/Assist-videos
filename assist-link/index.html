Assist Link. Three apps. One umbrella.

Every towing station has quiet weeks. Assist Link is how planned transport jobs reach them: one-off customers book in Assist Work, dispatch centers offer agreements in Assist Connect, and every job lands in Assist Dispatch, where it is accepted, done and invoiced.

  • For one-off customers · conceptAssist WorkAssist's own automatic dispatch center. Customers without a center of their own book a single job; they pick a station or Assist finds the nearest.
  • For dispatch centers · conceptAssist ConnectLogistics and rental companies create an agreement and offer it to the stations. The stations accept, and the jobs start flowing.
  • For the stations · todayAssist DispatchThe app stations already use. Requests come in, the station accepts, and the driver gets it done. Other systems plug in through an API.
