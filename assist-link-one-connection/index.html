Assist Link. One connection. All of Norway.

Stations have trucks and people – the jobs just don't come. Assist Link connects those who have jobs with the stations that can do them. The film explains the three apps, why Swipload is the first step, and how Assist earns from both sides.

Download: English (3:52) · Norsk (2:41)

  • For the stations · todayAssist DispatchThe app stations already use. Jobs are received, carried out and invoiced here, and shared with friend stations.
  • Step 1 · nowSwipload → DispatchA generic API sends Swipload orders straight into the station's Dispatch, with status and POD back. One app for the driver.
  • Step 2 · next releaseSwipload in ConnectSwipload becomes a dispatch centre in Assist Connect and sends jobs to more stations. This is where the volume comes.
  • Revenue · both sidesHow Assist earns2% integration fee from the station, Connect subscription from customers, 4–6% brokerage when Assist brings the customer, and new Dispatch licences.
