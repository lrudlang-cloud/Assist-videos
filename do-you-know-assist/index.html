Do you know Assist?

Built for towing stations – from the moment the phone rings until the invoice is sent. In 76 seconds the film shows how a job comes in, is set up and carried out, and what we are building now. The Assist screens are shown in Norwegian.

  • The job comes inBooking formRegular customers book themselves from your own website. The booking lands straight in Assist.
  • The job comes inJob interpreterAn AI agent reads the message from the dispatch center, looks up the vehicle in the vehicle register and creates the job in seconds.
  • The job is set upService templatesPick the type of job, and the work description and checklist come with it. Six ready-made templates that you adapt yourself.
  • The job is carried outDriver flowThe driver gets the job on the phone. One big button always shows the next step.
  • The job is carried outChecklist on the jobPhotos, a text to the customer and the deductible. Nothing is forgotten.
  • Being built nowAssist LinkStations, dispatch centers and other clients working together, also across the border. Jobs you would not otherwise have had, straight into Assist Dispatch.
